# Hacking With SwiftUI - Rock, Paper, Scissors

## Description:
This was a Hacking With SwiftUI challange based off what I learnt on the other projects (Conversions, GuessTheFlag, WeSplit).

The challenge was to create a rock, paper, scissors game. The key tasks were -
1. Each turn the computer will randomly pick Rock, Paper or Scissors.
2. Each turn the computer will randomly select between the player winning or losing.
3. Once the computer has selected their move (rock, paper or scissors) and if the player should win or lose. The player must tap the correct move to win or lose the turn.
4. A point is awarded if chosen correctly or lost if they choose incorrectly.
5. The game ends after 10 questions, from there an alert is shown with their score and a chance to play again.

E.g., If the computer chose "Rock" and "Win", the player will need to chose "Paper" (player would win), but if the player chose "Scissors", the player would have lost.

## What I learn't
1. @State properties are incredibly powerful in SwiftUI, I feel like I should default to @State whenever I know it's going to change or something to be kept an eye on (e.g., Alerts).
2. Continued use of Stacks, getting a clearer picture to how it's used effectively.
